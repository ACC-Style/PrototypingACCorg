Content ops

  1. Edit the FAQ content in _data/annual_meeting/faqs/: spokes.csv, groups.csv, and items.csv.
  2. Rebuild / refresh the site so Jekyll picks up the CSV changes.
  3. Select the spoke for the page you want to pull content from.
  4. Copy the needed content into Sitecore CMS richtext areas.
  5. Preview spokes under /AnnualMeeting/faqs/.

Status values

  • ready — renders on the page and in the CMS blocks below.
  • hold — not ready yet; skipped everywhere.
  • delete — skipped everywhere and listed for removal below.
